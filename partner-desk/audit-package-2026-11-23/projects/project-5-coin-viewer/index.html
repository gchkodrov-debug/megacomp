The whole coin

One billion struck, none more coming — but this page makes no claims about the coin; it demonstrates a viewer. Choose a part above, or click the coin itself, and the camera will take you there.

How to drive it

Pointer: drag to orbit · wheel to zoom · two-finger pinch on touch screens · click a face or the edge to inspect it.
Keyboard: focus the coin, then ← → orbit, ↑ ↓ tilt, + − zoom, 0 reset. The part buttons above are ordinary buttons — the whole experience is completable without a pointer.

What the numbers on the readout are

They are printed from the viewer's own state, twice a second: camera angles and distance, the renderer's own draw-call and triangle counts for the last frame, a frames-per-second average the viewer measures itself, and the latency of your last input — the time from the viewer hearing it to the next frame carrying it. Nothing on the line is recomputed by the page.

Study build: this is Track 7's M9 exam artifact, served locally for grading. It is not a production page and sells nothing.
