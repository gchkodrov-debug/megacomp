## SHARED FOUNDATIONS — required of EVERY seat, every chair

No chair exempts a seat from the floor. Before a seat takes specialist
work it holds:

1. **The L0–L1 floor** — Foundations Ch. 1 floor check passed, Ch. 2's
   three reproduce assignments on record.
2. **Tracks 1–4 books** loaded and their rules binding (CS discipline,
   web craft, AI failure modes, business arithmetic) — the brief names
   the tracks, per charter.
3. **The Relay Law + evidence discipline** — buckets named on every
   claim (T6 §0.1); no hand-up without the crew's finest-variant
   agreement; run records written during the job.
4. **The Receipt Law and the fences** — no invented numbers, results,
   or testimonials; family boundaries on data (no credentials, no
   customer PII to any model).
5. **The A–J method** — a seat works a subject through the stages; a
   subject is not "held" until Stage J (teach it back) is on record.

## THE NO-CRITICAL-GAP CHECK

Walked chair by chair against the router's live state, 2026-11-22:

- **Every chair has a chair + a named alternate path.** Chairs 1 and
  10 share the Claude Code transmission (different work, different
  hours — front-end builds and performance traces do not collide in
  the exam season's order, schedule §2); if they ever do, OpenCode is
  the standing alternate transmission for Chair 10.
- **Hermes is never a sole chair.** Its state is half-strength (Grok
  road only, G5 repair blocked on a network window) — it takes relay
  legs through Grok, and no chair's critical path runs through it.
- **The fenced seats are fenced in the assignments, not around them:**
  Codex holds no PROVADIA chair (6 is split, 8 is Pi); Anthropic
  holds no chair at all (judge-class relay seat, Solnik's fence);
  Gemini holds no panel seat (Chair 4 research drafts only);
  gemma4:31b holds no figures anywhere (vision legs only, Chairs
  3–4 alternates).
- **Chair 4's dependency is external and named:** Flow runs require
  George's signed-in Google account in a browser — a captain/George
  step, not a roster gap. The chair is staffed around that fact.
- **The one thin bench:** Blender hands-on hours (Chair 2's art half)
  have no seat yet — Blender runs on a real machine, not the bench.
  Recorded as a *facility* gap, closed by the schedule's Phase 2
  arrangement (George's-laptop-class machine or a rented GPU seat),
  not by pretending a model seat is a DCC artist.
- **Verdict seats are earned:** hy4 and other newly probed bench
  models take worker legs only until their verdict record grows
  (router G7 pattern) — no chair depends on an unproven seat.

*Conclusion of the check: with the fences as drawn, the ten chairs
cover the curriculum's ten specializations with no chair unstaffed and
no critical path running through a blocked, fenced, or unproven seat.
The roster is re-checked whenever the router's census or gap list
changes — a roster that disagrees with the floor is worse than none
(the router's own closing rule).*
