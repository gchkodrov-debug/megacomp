ONEJOBKITS — PLATINUM PRISTINE WEBSITE DNA

Universal Quality Classification, Creative Engineering & Elite Production Standards

TO: Captain, Muse, and the Entire OneJobKits Crew

FROM: George, Founder

DIRECTIVE: Establish a universal website-quality classification system and master the engineering required to produce the highest-quality original websites possible.

STANDARD: Bentley × Rolls-Royce × Lamborghini × Nike × SpaceX levels of attention to detail, innovation, craftsmanship, and presentation.

These brands are benchmarks for ambition and quality, not templates to copy.

⸻

PART 1 — THE FOUNDER'S VISION

I do not want ordinary websites.

I do not want generic templates with a few animations.

I do not want websites that merely look expensive in screenshots.

I want websites that feel like complete, extraordinary digital experiences.

Every major component must work together as one unified organism.

The design, animation, cinematography, 2D graphics, 3D graphics, typography, interaction, performance, accessibility, engineering, and product functionality must operate as one integrated system.

This is our Website DNA Principle.

Think of a luxury performance vehicle.

A powerful engine alone does not make a great vehicle.

It also requires the transmission, steering, suspension, braking, electronics, structural engineering, and precise assembly.

A failure in one critical component compromises the entire machine.

Our websites must follow the same principle.

A spectacular 3D animation does not compensate for broken navigation.

Beautiful typography does not compensate for poor performance.

Exceptional visual design does not compensate for an inaccessible interface.

Every critical system must work.

Our objective is to create original digital products that demonstrate extraordinary craftsmanship while remaining genuinely useful, technically sound, and commercially valuable.

⸻

PART 2 — UNIVERSAL WEBSITE QUALITY LEVELS

Establish the following six-level classification system.

LEVEL 1 — BASIC

Quality band: 0–49/100

An ordinary functional website.

Characteristics:

* Simple layouts.
* Basic typography.
* Standard navigation.
* Minimal animation.
* Limited visual originality.
* Conventional content sections.
* Basic responsive behavior.
* Little custom art direction.

A basic website may be perfectly suitable for a simple business.

However, it does not represent our premium cinematic standard.

LEVEL 2 — MEDIUM

Quality band: 50–64/100

A competent, professionally assembled website.

Characteristics:

* Consistent layout.
* Responsive components.
* Reasonable typography.
* Clean navigation.
* Basic interactive elements.
* Some customization.
* Modest motion design.
* Generally professional appearance.

The website works, but its visual and interactive experience remains conventional.

LEVEL 3 — GREAT

Quality band: 65–79/100

A polished professional website.

Characteristics:

* Strong visual identity.
* Good typography and composition.
* Thoughtful user experience.
* Refined transitions.
* Custom design elements.
* Consistent responsive layouts.
* Well-integrated animations.
* Good content hierarchy.
* Professional engineering.

This level should feel noticeably more refined than an ordinary commercial website.

LEVEL 4 — HIGH-END

Quality band: 80–89/100

A sophisticated, original digital experience.

Characteristics:

* Distinctive art direction.
* Strong visual storytelling.
* Advanced motion design.
* Carefully choreographed interactions.
* Custom illustrations or 3D elements where appropriate.
* High-quality cinematic assets.
* Premium typography.
* Excellent responsive design.
* Strong performance engineering.
* Consistent attention to detail.

This is where the website begins to feel like a carefully directed digital production rather than a collection of pages.

LEVEL 5 — PLATINUM

Quality band: 90–95/100

An exceptional, highly refined website.

Characteristics:

* Original creative direction.
* Exceptional visual composition.
* Cinematic storytelling.
* Sophisticated 2D and 3D integration.
* Advanced animation engineering.
* Custom visual effects.
* Carefully controlled camera choreography.
* Seamless transitions.
* Exceptional interaction quality.
* Premium asset production.
* Rigorous performance optimization.
* Professional accessibility.

* Complete testing and documentation.

Every major visual and functional system must feel deliberately engineered.

LEVEL 6 — PLATINUM PRISTINE

Quality band: 96–100/100

Our highest internal classification.

This is the OneJobKits flagship standard.

The website must demonstrate:

* Exceptional originality.
* Outstanding art direction.
* Cinematic visual storytelling.
* World-class visual polish.
* Seamless 2D and 3D integration where relevant.
* Sophisticated animation choreography.

Critical quality gates must also pass.

PART 4 — NON-NEGOTIABLE QUALITY GATES

A website cannot qualify as Platinum or Platinum Pristine if it has any unresolved critical failure.

Critical failures include:

1. Broken core functionality.
2. Unusable navigation.
3. Major accessibility barriers.
4. Serious security vulnerabilities.
5. Unacceptable performance on supported devices.
6. Missing essential content.
7. Broken mobile layouts.
8. Unlicensed or unauthorized assets.
9. Misleading product functionality.
10. Incomplete installation or customization instructions for commercial templates.
11. Major visual or interaction defects.
12. Unresolved browser compatibility failures within the supported matrix.

Critical failures must be corrected before certification.

A website scoring 98/100 with a serious security vulnerability is not Platinum Pristine.

A website with extraordinary 3D visuals but unusable mobile navigation is not Platinum Pristine.

No amount of visual polish can override a failed critical gate.

PART 11 — OBJECTIVE PERFORMANCE REQUIREMENTS

Platinum Pristine quality requires measured performance.

Use Google's Core Web Vitals as baseline user-experience indicators:

* Largest Contentful Paint: target 2.5 seconds or less.
* Interaction to Next Paint: target 200 milliseconds or less.
* Cumulative Layout Shift: target 0.1 or less.

Evaluate these at the 75th percentile of real-user experiences where sufficient data exists.

Also establish project-specific targets for:

* Animation frame pacing.
* GPU memory usage.
* JavaScript execution.
* Initial page weight.
* Image decoding.
* 3D asset loading.
* Input responsiveness.
* Mobile thermal behavior.
* Battery consumption.
* Fallback performance.

Set device-specific rendering targets appropriate to the audience.

Do not automatically fail a product for missing 60 FPS on every device, but do not excuse visible jank or unusable interaction.

Define and test the supported device matrix.
