## What the sitting was

The documentation examination for §26 row 16: a builder
working from the Project 12 Kit's documents under a
different brief from the demonstration storefront's.
The preserved build is **"Hearth №1"** — a cast-iron
skillet launch page for "Ember & Oak", built on the
Kit's Template F (configurator): a genuinely different
product from the demo's lamp storefront. Its config
discloses on its face that its media are the demo's
lamp assets borrowed as labelled temporary stand-ins.

## The grading's statement

The preserved build demonstrates that the Kit's
documentation sufficed to build a different,
instrument-passing product page. The sitting's exam
conditions — fresh builder, documents alone, timed,
zero questions — are **unevidenced**, and this
memorandum does not attest them. Compare the Phase 4
standard (`grading-phase4/EXAM_ATTEMPT_1–4.md`: fresh
builder named as such, UTC start/end, elapsed to the
second, every gap dispositioned): the Phase 5 sitting
was run to a lower filing standard than the house set
one phase earlier, and that shortfall is the phase's,
recorded here rather than repaired by invention.
