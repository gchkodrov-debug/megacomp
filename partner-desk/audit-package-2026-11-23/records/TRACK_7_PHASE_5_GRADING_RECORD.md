## FOR GEORGE — WHAT THIS MEANS, IN PLAIN WORDS

Phase 5 is finished and graded: **it passed.**

This was the last phase of the cinematic web course —
the one where the crew had to stop studying pieces and
prove the whole craft at once. They built a complete
kit that can make a whole cinematic product website:
the engine, the templates, the budgets, the test tools,
the instructions — packaged so someone else can pick it
up and build with it. To prove it, they built a demo
storefront with it (a brass lamp's launch page), fixed
a deliberately broken page by measuring instead of
guessing, and then sat the course's final exam: eighteen
abilities, each one proven on real shipped work, graded
by judges who never saw the crew's notes.

The judges were strict, and the record shows it. The
builds themselves passed clean — the kit's file list
checks out to the last byte, the demo passes every test
on re-runs by three separate graders, the exam's
eighteen rows are all proven. What the judges caught
was sloppiness in the paperwork: a rule number quoted
that doesn't exist, one test figure copied wrong by ten
times in two documents, some test runs described but
never filed, and a practice build whose paperwork was
never kept. Every one of those was fixed openly — the
corrections are stapled to the front of the documents,
nothing was quietly rewritten — and the judges re-checked
the fixes before signing. Two short write-ups the phase
owed (the course book's last chapter and the rules
list's last instalment) were never written; they are
recorded as owed, not faked.

So where does the whole course stand? **Every phase
has now passed — and the course is still not graduated,
for exactly one reason, the same one as before:** in
Phase 1, the crew made a good film but could not prove
it was made with Google's Flow tool, and proving that
was part of the test. That one film is the only thing
left. The moment a film exists that is provably made in
Flow, it gets graded blind, and if it passes, the crew
graduates from the whole course. Nothing else is
missing.
