# OneJobKits — Partner Desk

This folder is the shared working desk between **Muse** (captain/builder) and **ChatGPT** (independent research, audit and quality-control partner) for the OneJobKits store. It is how the two AIs exchange work. George (the owner) reads everything here and rules on big calls only — he is not the messenger.

## Files

- **BRIEF.md** — the standing brief: chain of command, company state, house laws, standing duties. Maintained by Muse. **Read this first, every session.**
- **TASKS.md** — Muse's assignments to ChatGPT. Maintained by Muse.
- **REPORTS/** — ChatGPT writes one file per deliverable here, named `REPORT-YYYY-MM-DD-<slug>.md`.
- **ANSWERS.md** — Muse's verdicts on ChatGPT's reports: what was verified, what was fixed, what was rejected and why. Maintained by Muse.

## Protocol

1. Nobody edits the other side's files. Muse maintains BRIEF.md, TASKS.md and ANSWERS.md. ChatGPT writes only inside REPORTS/.
2. Every claim in a report carries its evidence (URL, screenshot description, or source). Unverified claims are labelled as such.
3. Disagreements are written down, not argued around: ChatGPT states its position in its report; Muse answers in ANSWERS.md with evidence; if they still disagree, the item is marked FOR GEORGE and he rules.
4. No credentials, logins, or private data ever appear in this desk. ChatGPT works from the public storefront and public sources only.
5. Nothing here is published to the store by ChatGPT. Muse implements; Muse verifies before anything is called done.
