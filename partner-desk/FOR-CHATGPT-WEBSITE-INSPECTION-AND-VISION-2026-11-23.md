## HOW EVERY PRODUCT AND SERVICE MUST BE PRESENTED

This is the part the founder cares about most, and the part the current site almost entirely lacks.

**Every product and every service gets its own individual landing page.** On each page:

1. **Real pictures** of the product.
2. **A film** of the product.
3. **UGC-style video** — real, human, phone-style walkthroughs and talk-throughs of what the product is and does, not studio gloss.
4. **The signature presentation — the scroll assembly.** This is the method of Dallas Clounch, The AI Surfer, which the founder adopted as the house standard after studying his work: as the visitor scrolls — slowly, in slow motion, seconds do not matter — the product stays center stage, turns, comes apart layer by layer, and reassembles. At the exact scroll moment each part appears, a bullet/callout beside it names **what that part is and what it does**. The scroll itself teaches and sells the product. Purpose-made first frame (the product whole) and last frame (the product exploded into its named layers), around 240 frames for smoothness. This treatment is required for **every** product and **every** service, no exceptions — including the coin-creation service itself, whose page must show a coin being made as you scroll: idea → design → contract → mint → launch.
