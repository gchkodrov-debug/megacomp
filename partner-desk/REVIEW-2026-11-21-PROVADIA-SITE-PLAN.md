## THE SHARED DESIGN LAW (every page, no exceptions)
- **Palette (exact):** field #141210 · antique gold #C9A227 · pale gold #FFEFC1 · dark bronze #6B4A12 · deep brown #3D2A08 · salt white #F4EFE6. **No purple, no teal, no neon, no generic crypto styling.**

- **Every page is a PLACE in the same town** — same light, same palette, same coin — with living motion (slow turns, drifting light, parallax, reveal-on-scroll). - **Navigation on every page:** six doors — The Town · The Record · The Forum · The Knock · The Ecosystem · About Us → index · /record/ · /agora/ · /knock/ · /ecosystem/ · /about/.
- **THE RECEIPT LAW (the fence):** Genspark may print ONLY the facts the prompts give it. Every future live number is a `data-live` placeholder showing either the TRUE current value (the genuine zeros) or an honest waiting line. No invented prices, counts, charts, links, handles, or testimonials — anywhere, including "sample" data.
- **The honesty sentence, verbatim, in every page's footer/foundation:** "PROVADIA is a place, not an investment. Holding it names you a Bricklayer in the town registry and gives you a vote on the Wall's fate — and nothing else: no redemption, no yield, no rights, no expected return."
- **Banned everywhere:** "24/7 / around the clock / never sleeps" claims · "guaranteed / endorsed / backed / vetted" · any return/yield promise · any content about secrets or mystery reveals (out of scope for this build entirely).
