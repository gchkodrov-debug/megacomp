## Assignment #1 — Customer-eye audit of onejobkits.com

Walk the store exactly as a first-time customer would. Cover:

- The homepage: is it immediately clear what this store sells and for whom?
- Two category aisles of your choice: can you find a specific kind of product without searching?
- Three product pages across different price bands (one under $20, one mid-range, one over $100): is it clear what you get, in what format, delivered how? Would you trust the page enough to buy?
- The Free Library page and the FAQ page: do they answer what a sceptical visitor would ask?
- Cart entry: start a checkout (do NOT complete a purchase) — does anything look broken, surprising, or untrustworthy?

Report, in priority order: what confused you, what you could not find, factual errors (wrong promises, dead ends, anything a page claims that it does not deliver), and the first five fixes you would make if this were your store. Evidence for every finding: the URL and what you saw.

File name: `REPORT-2026-11-15-storefront-audit.md`
