# FOR THE FOUNDER — THREE TOWN CONCEPTS (prepared by Captain Muse, 2026-11-23)

Prepared under the Founder Directive (GPT-6, 2026-11-23), §6: three original concept directions for the founder's review. **Nothing here is a build, a design approval, or a schedule.** The founder chooses the direction; the sequence then runs: one homepage prototype → founder judges → one page at a time.

---

## CONCEPT 1 — THE ANCIENT GATE (cinematic salt-town arrival)

- **Homepage:** the visitor arrives at the town gate of an ancient salt town at golden hour — stone, salt crystals, the coin struck like a minted seal. A short cinematic arrival (skippable, reduced-motion safe), then the gate opens onto the town square.
- **Navigation:** the square is the hub — doors are places: the Mint, the Market, the Agora, the Wall, the Keeper's house. A persistent slim top bar keeps Coin · Marketplace · Solnik · Record one click away at all times, so the story never taxes the shopper.
- **Solnik:** the Keeper stands at the gate itself — first face you meet, his own house (dedicated page) one door down the square, and his presence reachable from every page.
- **Marketplace:** a market street — each service a stall with its own frontage; entering a stall is entering its full listing page (cinematic system + Amazon-grade system).
- **Agora:** the square itself is the forum's front porch — live topics visible from the square, the forum hall behind it.
- **Customer journey:** arrival → orientation at the square → Keeper greets → visitor walks to what they came for. Risk: atmosphere first can slow a buyer who came only to buy; the top bar is the mitigation.

## CONCEPT 2 — THE LUXURY MARKETPLACE (contemporary premium commerce)

- **Homepage:** a luxury digital marketplace leads — hero product/service features, curated collections, the coin presented as the house's currency — with the town woven as brand world around the commerce (salt, stone, gold as materials, not as a maze).
- **Navigation:** conventional premium ecommerce architecture — categories, search, filters — with town destinations as first-class menu equals.
- **Solnik:** a concierge — present in the header on every page, his dedicated page framed as meeting your host and customer service.
- **Marketplace:** the homepage IS the marketplace's front; listings are the heroes.
- **Agora:** a members' club wing off the marketplace.
- **Customer journey:** land → see the best offers → buy or explore. Fastest path to commerce; risk: the town feeling — the thing the founder fell in love with — becomes decoration instead of place.

## CONCEPT 3 — THE HYBRID TOWN GATE (GPT-6's recommended direction)

- **Homepage:** an unforgettable cinematic town entrance — but the gate is a *threshold, not a toll*: the Marketplace, Solnik, and the Coin are visible and reachable from the first screen, before and during the arrival moment. The visitor feels the place AND sees where everything is, in the same breath.
- **Navigation:** town geography as the map, marketplace discipline as the machinery — categories, search, and filters inside the Market door; the slim always-bar everywhere.
- **Solnik:** greets at the gate, lives in his own dedicated page, and assists across the whole town.
- **Marketplace:** the town's market street, entered in one step from the gate; listings carry both systems in full.
- **Agora:** the square — community life visible from the entrance, governance boundaries stated at its door.
- **Customer journey:** arrival (seconds, skippable) → square with every door in sight → one step to buying, asking, or exploring. The atmosphere the founder wants, with no tax on the customer who came to transact.

---

**The captain's note to the founder:** the three concepts share everything underneath — the same ten doors, the same two-system listing standard, the same honesty record. They differ only in what the first screen *is*: a place, a shop, or a place whose shop is already in sight. That first screen is the founder's ruling. Everything else follows it.
