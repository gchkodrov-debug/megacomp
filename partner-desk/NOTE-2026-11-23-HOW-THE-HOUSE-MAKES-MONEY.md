## 1. How customers pay us

Every listing's buy area carries three rails side by side:

- **Card** — via Stripe hosted checkout.
- **PayPal** — hosted checkout.
- **Crypto** — SOL, or PROVADIA itself.

**The discount rule:** paying directly in **PROVADIA earns 20% off, flat**. SOL and other crypto pay full price. Card and PayPal pay full price.

## 2. What happens behind the counter

Whatever rail the customer uses, the house converts the proceeds **into PROVADIA** — at receipt, or in a disclosed daily batch. The house's treasury is held in its own coin. This is by design, not by accident: every sale, whatever the customer paid with, ends as PROVADIA behind the counter.

## 3. How the money splits

- **Product and service income: 80 / 20** — 80% is the founder's income; 20% goes to the town pot.
- **Coin creator fees: 80 / 20** — 80% is the founder's disclosed income (the only way he makes money from the coin); 20% goes to the **Golden Wall** forever — physical 1-gram gold bars in a named third-party vault. The gold is never backing, never redeemable; the Wall counter runs in grams and bricks only.

## 4. How the founder actually gets paid

The loop closes in reverse: when the founder needs dollars, he converts PROVADIA back to fiat — sells through the market/exchange route into his bank. Money comes in on any rail, becomes PROVADIA behind the counter, and comes back out as dollars when he draws his share. That is the whole circuit: **any rail in → PROVADIA in the middle → dollars out when he gets paid.**
