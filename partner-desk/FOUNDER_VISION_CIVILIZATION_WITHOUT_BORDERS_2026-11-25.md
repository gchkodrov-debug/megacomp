**The Expansion** — New worlds, institutions and experiences

The creative possibilities are enormous. The practical limits will be engineering, security, legal compliance, operating costs and genuine human demand.

I would preserve this thought as another dimension of FVR-30:

**A civilization without geographical borders, rather than a website without page limits.**

I wouldn't change tonight's construction orders. I wouldn't start another project. I would simply make sure that the foundations we are building today do not unnecessarily restrict what PROVADIA could become tomorrow.

The more we discuss this, the more I understand why you insist that every door must lead into its own living world.

You're not imagining pages anymore.

You're imagining territories.
