PROVADIA panel — SALT, the in-world currency. George's design (2026-11-19): to buy, contribute, or trade INSIDE the digital world, a participant needs SALT — and SALT is obtained by trading the main coin (PROVADIA, the "golden coin," the real tradable token) for it. Hold the main coin → trade it for SALT → SALT is what participates inside the town.

CAPTAIN'S SHAPING (grade this):
- SALT is a ONE-WAY door: main coin → SALT → spent inside the world. SALT never trades back into the coin, is never sold for money, never has a market price. Spent, not cashed.
- SALT is the town's own ledger unit (the town keeps the SALT book, like it keeps the citizens' registry) — NOT a second blockchain token. $0 to run, no speculation surface.
