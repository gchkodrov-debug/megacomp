| Plausibility Illusion | Feeling that events occurring inside that world are believable |

## 5. How the illusion of another dimension is manufactured

The secret is not simply generating more beautiful videos. It is coordinating perspective, scale, light, sound, motion, physics and character behavior.

Everything must agree with everything else.

For example, when Solnik walks beside molten gold, the golden light should reflect on his body. When he walks behind the press, the press should obscure him. When machinery strikes, the sound should originate from the machinery.

These seemingly small details collectively create believability.
