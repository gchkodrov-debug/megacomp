- Scroll assemblies (~240-frame mirrored journeys) — CHANGED AS A TOOL, NOT AS A DESIGN: (i) EXTEND lengthens a good scroll journey by 3–10s without re-rolling the whole clip — e.g. an 8s master + 3s Extend at 720p costs 9 credits versus 24 for a new clip, and adds ~72 frames at 24 fps (90 at 30 fps) of unique journey before the mirror, reducing visible repetition. Keep the original master exported, catalogued and backed up outside the account BEFORE editing; never edit the only copy. Re-verify frame count, continuity and Leg 7 sound after every edit.
- Mirrored returns — UNCHANGED for all 37 films. A mirrored return is editorial (reverse/mirrored frames in the scroll assembly), not a generation. Extend does not replace it; it gives the outbound journey more unique frames before the mirror. Omni does NOT automatically generate a true return journey (camera travelling back along the same path). A true generated return — e.g. a second Frames-to-Video with swapped Frame 1/Frame 2, or a conversational edit asking for the camera move reversed — is UNPROVEN for camera continuity and physics and must be piloted on one film before any film's mirrored return is replaced by it.
