    A) What Is VERIFIED About His Visual Flashcard Method
    
    Core 4-Beat Card Pedagogy (Picture → Question → Short Explanation → Final Takeaway):
    
    Every card isolates one single concept per visual frame rather than dumping a multi-paragraph textbook wall.
    
    Beat 1 (The Visual Anchor / Picture): A single, high-contrast central visual metaphor or deconstructed diagram that anchors memory before a single sentence is read.
    
    Beat 2 (The Hook Question): A direct, plain-English question ("Why does X happen?" / "What actually stops Y?") that triggers active recall instead of passive skimming.
    
    Beat 3 (The Short Explanation): A tightly constrained 2–3 sentence plain-language breakdown (≤ 45 words) explaining the mechanism without jargon.
    
    Beat 4 (The Final Takeaway): A bold, one-line rule-of-thumb summary ("The Rule:" / "Takeaway:") that the learner carries away permanently.
    
    Visual Engineering Principles:
    
    One Focal Object Per Card: Dark or clean high-contrast studio ground, generous negative space around the central object, and zero visual clutter.
    
    Spatial Deconstruction (Show, Don't Just Tell): Complex systems are broken into 3–5 labeled visual parts with clean callout pointers rather than abstract bullet lists.
    
    Swipe / Flip Rhythm: Designed natively for 9:16 vertical mobile carousels/reels and interactive card decks where each tap or swipe advances one atomic concept in < 15 seconds.
