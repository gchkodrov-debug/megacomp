# CAPTAIN'S INDEPENDENT EXAMINATION — "DISCOVERY OF SECRETS" RESEARCH PROPOSAL
Muse, captain · 2026-11-27 ~22:50 CT · Research only. The founder asked to be challenged; this challenges.

## VERDICT IN ONE LINE
The instinct is right and the strongest version is already half-built — but as a separate game economy it would be an expensive distraction today. Adopt the discovery layer as a principle of the existing build; gate every reward mechanic behind measured conversion evidence; build no new department.

## WHAT IS GENUINELY STRONG
1. Precedent is real. Roblox × Shopify proves discovery-commerce inside playable worlds at scale (97.8M daily users). Explore-to-discover is not a fantasy mechanic.
2. Our marginal cost is unusually low. PROVADIA already IS walkable worlds with cinematic product reveals (FVR-38). A normal store bolting on a game pays full price; we mostly pay for connective tissue — secrets, paths, challenges — inside worlds already owed.
3. Learn-to-unlock fits the catalogue honestly. Our products are knowledge and tools; a challenge that teaches a real skill is a truthful demo of the product it unlocks toward. Teaching is not a gimmick for this house; it is the merchandise.
4. Proof-of-achievement has foundations already built: Genesis (sealed, $0), Golden Wall bricks, Gatehouse — achievement collectibles extend existing work instead of inventing a new chain presence.

## THE CHALLENGES (where I push back)
1. THE AUDIENCE QUESTION IS THE WHOLE GAME, and the research admits it is unanswered. Roblox's crowd buys merchandise inside worlds they already inhabit for fun. Our buyers — traders, contractors, resellers — arrive with a job problem and a wallet, not a weekend. Precedent proves the mechanic; it proves nothing about OUR catalogue converting explorers into software customers. Until a small measured pilot answers it (do explorers convert better than direct visitors?), every larger claim is hope.
2. AXIE'S LESSON CUTS DEEPER THAN "TOKEN DEPENDENCE." Its economy required perpetual new-player inflow to pay earlier players — a structure, not a bug. Any reward with cash value inherits that gravity. Earn-to-save included: a discount is cash value; discounts get farmed, accounts get multiplied, codes get resold (our own STOREDNA25 is capped for exactly this reason). Bots and farming are not a risk to manage later; they are the first thing a reward economy manufactures.
3. EARN-TO-SAVE IS THE DANGEROUS MODEL. It trains customers to never pay full price and punishes the honest buyer who does. If it ever exists: account-bound, non-transferable, small, one per genuine achievement, expiring — and LAST in sequence, behind evidence, not first.
4. SEQUENCE IS THE REAL COST. Tonight the house stands at 0 READY products under the six-element standard; the OS was authorized hours ago; #140 and the assembly are mid-flight; the benches are capped. A gaming department does not compete with our money — it competes with the same three partners' attention. "Continue existing construction without interruption" is the right instinct, but a new department interrupts by existing.
5. PLATFORM AND RULES FRICTION IS REAL. Roblox's own Shopify channel excludes digital products — the biggest success story does not actually sell what we sell. Tradable achievement collectibles tied to rewards attract securities/gambling-adjacent scrutiny in some jurisdictions and platform-policy risk everywhere. Sealed, non-tradable achievement proofs avoid most of this; tradability is where the trouble lives.

## THE BIGGER OPPORTUNITY (my better-vision candidate)
The strong version is not "a game added to a store." It is that EXPLORATION ITSELF becomes the store's native mechanic — the secrets were already in the founding vision (hidden chambers, the Knock Chamber, Solnik guiding, scrolls that unfurl when chosen). "Discovery of Secrets" as the name for the exploration layer of the civilization we already owe: visitors who explore deeper meet more of the catalogue, presented by the Living Product Listing standard; challenges teach the skill the product serves; achievement proofs (sealed, non-tradable) mark real accomplishments in the town. That version costs little beyond the current build, carries no token economy, no farming surface worth attacking, and deepens the one moat nobody can copy quickly — a store that is a place.

## RECOMMENDED GATES (if the founder adopts anything)
G1. Discovery/exploration layer: adopt as a DESIGN PRINCIPLE inside current construction (no new department, no new queue line).
G2. Learn-to-unlock: pilot inside ONE existing world with ONE product line; measure explorer-vs-direct conversion for a fixed window.
G3. Proof-of-achievement: sealed + non-tradable only, on the existing Genesis/Golden Wall foundations; tradability requires its own future ruling.
G4. Earn-to-save: DEFERRED until G2 produces conversion evidence; then only in the capped, bound, expiring form above.
G5. Any play-to-earn or token-reward mechanic: rejected outright — against the house's standing laws and Axie's evidence.
