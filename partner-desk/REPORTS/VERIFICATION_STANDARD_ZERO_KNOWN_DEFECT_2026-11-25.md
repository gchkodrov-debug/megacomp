# THE ZERO KNOWN-DEFECT RULE — Verification Standard Amendment (house law)

**Adopted:** founder's order 2026-11-25 ~07:20 CT, on ChatGPT's strengthening of the captain's draft amendment (Declared-Asset Completeness, written after the Mint D2 root-cause investigation). **Scope: BOTH civilizations — PROVADIA and OneJobKits — every product, the Software Factory, every verification seat. No partner, crew or verification seat may bypass these requirements.**

## The eight rules

1. **A known, unresolved defect automatically blocks a final PASS.** Severity labels describe a defect; they never excuse it. A verdict issued with a known-open defect on its record is not a PASS — it is a report of incompletion.
2. **Assigning a correction is not completing a correction.** Naming a crew, queueing a fix, or logging "for cleanup" changes nothing. A defect closes only when the correction is made and retested (rule 5).
3. **Local files are not proof of successful live deployment.** A file on disk, in a mirror, in a demo tree, or in a repository proves existence in that place only. Deployment is proven at the live address, on the production origin.
4. **Every declared commercial asset must be verified at its live address and visually confirmed within the actual customer experience** — rendered the way a customer summons it, on desktop and mobile. (This is the Declared-Asset Completeness gate, §6(g) of the Alive Listing Standard: live HTTP 200 at the declared URL + rendered confirmation + no known-open asset defect at any severity.)
5. **Every correction must be independently retested before the defect is closed.** The seat that fixed it does not close it; a different seat re-probes the live evidence.
6. **Missing evidence means UNVERIFIED, never PASS.** Absence of a check is not a passed check. Where the evidence pack does not contain the proof, the verdict says UNVERIFIED for that item.
7. **Any subsequent modification requires re-verification of the affected gates.** A change reopens the gates it touches; prior verdicts do not carry over a modification.
8. **Previous PASS verdicts issued under the defective standard must be reviewed before being relied upon.** A verdict is only as strong as the standard that produced it. Reliance on an unreviewed legacy PASS is prohibited for any new decision, replication, or publication.

## Enforcement

- A verification record that issues PASS while carrying a known-open defect is itself a defect, and is reported as one.
- "On disk" is struck as sufficient proof in every house standard (Alive Listing Standard §6(c) amended accordingly).
- Reference sweeps (every page-referenced file answering 200) and local-mirror probes are **necessary, never sufficient** evidence.
- The founder's final approval remains mandatory above every seat's verdict. A product can be 99.9% complete and still not be READY.
