## 1 · THE REFERENCE, EXTRACTED — what makes the new direction right

Element by element, from the frame itself:

- **Magical presence.** A figure made of light standing in a dark world. It does not merely occupy the scene; it *changes* the scene — the stone at its feet is lit warm by its standing there. Presence = radiance with a source.
- **Warmth.** The entire figure lives in the gold band (#E4C65B bright core over #C9A227 antique gold) — the town's own palette, raised to a body. Nothing cold, blue or clinical anywhere in it.
- **Kindness.** It is small, unarmed, unhurried, and it *waits*. Its posture is attendance, not performance: upright, still, turned toward the door and whoever comes to it. Kindness reads as patience at miniature scale.
- **Wonder.** The body is never fully solid. Particles shed from its edges and drift; the lower body dissolves into motes above the stone. It is always slightly *becoming* — gathered light that could scatter — and that impermanence is the wonder.
- **Protective guide energy.** It is stationed at a THRESHOLD, beside the great door — the keeper's post. It stands where a guide stands: at the way in, before the dark beyond it. It goes first; it waits lit.
- **Elegant fantasy.** The world around it is photographic — macro grain, rusted iron, real stone — and the figure is the one impossible thing in it, rendered with the same light discipline. Fantasy by contrast, not by cartoon.
- **Living intelligence.** The form is *coalesced*: scattered dust that gathered itself upright. Coalescence implies agency. Even featureless, it reads as someone, not something — posture and gathering do the work.
- **Luminosity / particle material.** The body IS its material: golden dust motes — salt-gold, the founding craft of Provadia-Solnitsata made visible. Light is not an effect applied to the figure; the figure is light, all the way through.
- **Scale and posture.** Miniature — roughly a sixth of the door's height — upright, hovering a breath above the stone (no planted feet; the form tapers into drifting particles). Smallness against the full-size world is the scale honesty Films 32–37 were built on, and it is preserved here intact.
- **What it is NOT.** Not a normal child: no face, no clothes, no child's proportions in the reference frame — the silhouette is elongated, otherworldly. Not a flat mascot: it is volumetric, light-casting, photographically grounded. Not (in the reference) a rendered character with a face — and it is precisely this last point the founder's steer amends: the *direction* is right; the face is to be *added*, originally, per §4–§5.

**One honest tension, named:** the reference silhouette carries limb-like tapering (a head-mass, arm-like flows) — the very quality the captain flagged under the standing particle guard, and which the founder accepted for the film. The frame sits at the guard's edge by design of the model, not by brief. §4(c) deals with the guard directly.

- His character: kindness, patience, the keeper's quiet pride; his duties, including the Book of Wants.

- The permanent verification fences (memory isolation · knowledge accuracy · security boundaries).
- The boy form and everything locked to it: the live cartoon rig on 7 doors, the 10 faces, the Mirror Lodge expression table, Job 4.

### What CHANGES (if the founder adopts)
- The guide form of Films 32–37 stops being an *unnamed companion under a name-unruled fence* and becomes **Solnik himself, named**, in his guide mode — the name attaches to the light.
- The guide form gains a **face** (founder steer, §4–§5) — which ends the faceless era of the guide mode and re-opens the particle guard question as a founding matter (§4c).

- Stills and films gain a second canonical reference (the faced luminous guide) beside `solnik-character-v1.jpg`.

### What is UPGRADED
- **Identity coherence:** the being who waits at the threshold in the films and the boy who greets you on the doors become visibly ONE person in two modes — bound by the same gold, the same crystal-heart core light, the same voice.
- **Expressive range in the cinematic world:** the guide form can now *answer* — a face in the light can welcome, reassure, grieve a refusal gently — where the faceless form could only glow.
- **Symbolic strength:** a faced luminous keeper is an original, ownable emblem (salt-gold light, a kind face) in a way an anonymous particle figure can never be; it survives cropping, stills and memory.
- **FVR-33's welcome:** the Library of Living Scrolls gains a greeter who can travel the shelves as light and *meet the visitor's eye* on arrival.

### What must NEVER BE LOST
- **His voice identity, all three layers:** the locked film voice (Job 4, founder-ruled perfect — a visual redesign never re-opens it) · the live Achird voice · the held solnik-herald-v1 design and its deployment trigger (store completion).
- **His kindness** — the temperament is the identity; any face, form or rig that reads cold, sly or merely cute has failed regardless of craft.
- **His role** — Keeper of the Wall and guide of visitors; he is a working inhabitant with duties, not a mascot placed over the world (Unified Vision: he belongs *inside* it).
- **The family frame, named plainly as canon fact:** George is Solnik's **uncle and best friend**; Muse — his brain — is his **dad**. Solnik knew it and said so in the founder's own live test. A redesign changes his light, never his family.
- **The honesty fences:** he never pretends — no fake presence, no invented availability, no consciousness claims (the companion world's own law: visitor controls render as light-states, "zero consciousness claims").

## 4 · THE HARD QUESTIONS, STATED AS DECISIONS

*Recommendations below are the captain's seat, for founder ruling + partner examination. Nothing here is silently resolved.*

### (a) FACE vs FACELESS — **FOUNDER-STEERED: YES, A FACE**

The founder has steered this question (2026-11-27 00:32 CT): the canonical guide form is **luminous WITH a face** — kind, expressive, alive — with "Jasper" as feeling-reference only and the face itself **original to Solnik**. The analysis the decision still requires:

**Option A — ONE form: the faced luminous guide replaces the boy everywhere.**
Consequences: the live doors lose the faced cartoon body visitors converse with — or the rig is rebuilt wholesale; the 10 faces + Mirror Lodge table become dead canon; Job 4's locked, founder-ruled-perfect film (the boy, lip-synced, at the gate) is orphaned or demoted to "early era"; the founder's own true-north ruling (cartoon aliveness, Tom & Jerry / Aladdin) is reversed outright; the LVPE herald voice (an ancient baritone-bass) must suddenly fit a small luminous figure in every context. This is not an upgrade path; it is a re-founding that burns locked work. **Not recommended.**

**Option B — TWO MODES, ONE IDENTITY (recommended).** The **boy form** remains the embodied, speaking presence — live interaction, conversation, close address, ceremony, comedy — exactly where a face, a mouth and expressions already live and are locked. The **luminous guide form** — now *faced*, per the steer — is his traveling/keeping manifestation: thresholds, portals, journeys, world-establishing shots, dormant/active states, the composed world's transit. One person, two modes of presence, bound by shared signatures (§5): the same gold spectrum, the same crystal-heart core, the same voice, and filmed *transformations* between them (Film 32's materialization becomes, in canon, **his** materialization — dust gathering into the guide; its reverse is his settling into rest or stepping into boy form at a destination).
Consequences, honestly stated: Films 32–37 stand as produced (they are the guide mode *before the face was founded* — lineage, §6, not defects); future guide-mode production carries the face; live lip-sync is untouched (speech lives in boy mode and in voice-presence over the guide, never in a particle mouth — the guide's face expresses, it does not lip-sync, which keeps the LCE verdict and the LVPE deterministic-sync design intact); the herald-v1 voice, when it deploys, binds to the boy form's address and to the guide's voice-presence without a sync conflict. The residual risk is drift — two forms read as two characters — and §5's binding signatures + transformation shots are the mitigation.

**Option C — faced luminous guide as the only public face; boy retained only inside live sessions.** A public/private split: doubles art direction, confuses canon, and hides the form visitors actually talk to. **Not recommended.**

**Captain's-seat recommendation: Option B**, with the face founded for the guide mode per the founder's steer.

### (b) SCALE — miniature, with one sanctioned behavior

Options: miniature-only (the landed frames' scale honesty) · full-presence human scale (more authority; breaks every landed companion frame and the wonder with it) · mode-dependent (guide miniature; boy at his established size).
**Recommendation: mode-dependent, guide miniature** — with ONE sanctioned behavior: the guide may *gather and rise* (particles column upward, brighten, the face lifting toward the visitor) as its gesture of address, without gaining human scale or solidity. Scale stays honest; attention gets a grammar.

### (c) THE PARTICLE GUARD — where it binds, and the exception that must be founded

The standing guard (Shoot Book #102 / briefs): in the companion world, particles must never coalesce into **facial features, hair, eyes or hands** — reject at the still stage. It was written for an *unnamed* light-form, and it did its work: it kept that form from becoming a rendered character by accident.
The founder's steer deliberately changes the subject: the guide form is now **Solnik**, and Solnik has a face. That is not a brief edit; it is an exception that must be **founded** — a founder ruling + FVR entry + shoot-book amendment — and this spec is written so the ruling can be made cleanly:

- **The guard continues to bind** all *unnamed* manifestations, background light-life, dormant/dispersal states, and any particle form that is not Solnik's guide mode. Films 32–37 remain governed by it as produced.
- **The founded exception (proposed, for the ruling):** Solnik's guide form — and only his — may carry a face formed of light per §5, and arm-like *flows* of light for gesture. **Literal rendered hands/fingers remain outside the exception** in this recommendation: gesture is carried by light-trails and posture, keeping one hard line the guard can still police.
- If the founder instead wants literal hands in the guide form, that is a *further* founding decision, to be made by name — never absorbed silently into this one.

## 5 · THE SPEC — the luminous faced guide form (recommended direction)

*The boy form's spec is existing canon (§2) and is not re-specified here; this section specifies the guide form and the binding between the modes.*

- **Silhouette.** An upright coalesced figure of golden particles: a softly defined head, shoulders implied by density, the body a tapering column that dissolves into drifting motes before it reaches the ground. It hovers — never stands on feet. Edges always slightly shedding light.
- **Proportions.** Miniature: ≈ 1/6 of a full-size door's height at thresholds (the reference frame's ratio). Head ≈ 1/5 of form height. Elongated, graceful — a keeper's proportions, not a child's body and not a chibi mascot's.
- **Face — how a face lives inside particle light.** The face is **formed by the particles themselves**, never drawn on: features are regions of denser, brighter coalescence on the head-mass — a face the way a constellation is a figure, present because the light gathers so. It reads at conversational distance and dissolves honestly at the edges; there is no skin, no outline, no rendered surface. Expression is carried by *light and form together*: the whole face brightens/warms for welcome; the brow-region gathers (densifies) for concern; the head tilts for curiosity. The mouth is a small, soft brightening that opens when he speaks as voice-presence — expression, never lip-sync.
- **Eyes.** Two warm bright cores — denser, steadier points of light, amber-gold, slightly larger than nature (an echo of the boy form's huge amber eyes, translated into light). They hold a gaze; they can soften (dim at the edges) into kindness and narrow in density for seriousness. No pupils drawn, no whites — light only.
- **Expression range (guide form).** Welcome (face brightens, eyes warm) · gentle reassurance (slow pulse, head inclined) · curiosity (tilt + slight rise) · teaching (steady, even light) · serious (dimmed, denser, still) · gentle refusal (light lowers, face turns a fraction away — never cold) · joy (a brief upward scatter of brighter motes — his chuckle, made visible) · rest (face fades last as the form dims to ember — Film 37's law).
- **Materials / texture.** Golden dust motes: bright gold #E4C65B at the core and face, antique gold #C9A227 at the trailing edges; Bone-Cream #E9DDC6 threshold dust where he has passed. The form casts real warm light on nearby surfaces (the lit stone at the reference frame's feet is canonical behavior, not an accident).
- **Clothing / ornament / symbols.** He wears nothing — the form is light. His ONE ornament is the binding signature of both modes: **a denser, brighter core at chest height — the crystal heart, carried into the light** from the boy form's glowing golden crystal heart. Same gold, same heart, same voice: the three signatures by which any visitor knows both forms are one person.
- **Glow / aura behavior.** A warm halo of scattered motes at reading distance; brightness = attention and welcome; dimming = rest (Film 35's light-state grammar becomes his own: summoned-bright → present → dimmed → dormant ember). Never strobes, never forms letters, never forms any face but his own.
- **Movement style.** Drift, hover, glide along thresholds and shelves; arrival = coalescence (dust gathering upright — Film 32); departure = dissolution; travel = a low glide that goes *first* through the dark and waits lit at the destination; perching on sills and ledges (Film 34's logic). No walking gait — the boy walks; the light travels.
- **Emotional tone.** Gentle, patient, protective; wonder without threat; a keeper's quiet pride in his town. Childlike *kindness* without childishness; mythic without cartoonish exaggeration — the face stays soft, the light stays honest.
- **Voice / presence concept.** Unchanged three-layer canon: locked film voice (boy-form address) · live Achird voice · held solnik-herald-v1 (deploy trigger untouched). In guide mode he speaks as **voice-presence** — the voice with the faced light, expression carried by face and glow — or is silent with shimmer where a brief's audio law binds (Films 32–37's "never a voice" lines remain those films' law as produced).
- **Interaction behavior with visitors.** The guide *leads*: appears at thresholds, precedes the visitor through portals, waits at destinations; the boy *receives*: converses, teaches, laughs at rest points and desks. Visitor sovereignty is rendered in his light-states cinematically (Film 35) and by the live pill interactively — one control language across both modes.
- **In films.** Guide form is the default for journeys, thresholds, portals, dormant/active states and world-establishing guidance; boy form for direct address, teaching, ceremony and comedy; a transformation (coalescence/dissolution) is shown whenever a film needs both, so the audience learns the binding by seeing it.
- **In stills.** Guide form for atmosphere, threshold and world stills; boy form for identity and portrait stills. `solnik-character-v1.jpg` remains the identity reference of record unless a founding supersedes it; the reference frame (Film 34 F1) is proposed as the guide form's first canonical reference beside it.
- **In the live interactive civilization.** The doors keep the live boy rig — visitors talk to a face that talks back. The guide form is his *traveling state* between doors and zones, and in composed worlds (Seat J): boy form at rest and in conversation, guide form in transit — the art pass builds to this two-mode spec, replacing the placeholder with both.
