### BAND D — Legal/regulatory clearance first

**D1. Anything leveraged or pooled:** perps, options, leveraged tokens, yield vaults or lending pools holding other people's money. CFTC v. Opyn ($250K), Deridex ($100K), and 0x ($200K) — 0x was charged **for leveraged tokens tradable through its front-end that it did not create**; counts included unregistered SEF/FCM and no BSA Customer Identification Program — https://www.cftc.gov/PressRoom/PressReleases/8774-23 · The SEC's Uniswap investigation closed Feb 2025 with no action — an enforcement-policy reprieve, **not a ruling that a DEX front-end is lawful.** Spot-only, non-custodial is the line; everything past it waits for counsel.
**D2. Selling price feeds others rely on** — our own oracle feeds that third parties liquidate against (a bad feed causes real liquidations — the Mango pattern), **and** building bespoke feeds (e.g. custom Switchboard feeds) where the client's liquidations will run on them: the liability theory is the same whether we "warrant" the feed or not, and this report does not let a wording distinction do work it rejected elsewhere. Integrating Chainlink/Pyth — *their* feeds, *their* liability — stays ordinary dev work under B2.
**D3. Identity verification as an issuer** — performing KYC/biometrics ourselves: BSA programme obligations, Illinois BIPA ($1,000–$5,000 per violation, private right of action — and this house is in Chicago), and World ID's regulator record (Spain/Portugal stop orders, Kenya deletion order). The integration-only version — wiring a regulated issuer's attestation (Solana Attestation Service / Human Passport) into a client's gate — is a legitimate add-on to A3/B3 work, never a standalone business.
**D4. Security/RWA token issuance platform** (the Bitbond tier, $499 asset tokens with transfer restrictions) — real money, real securities law; needs entity, KYC stack, and counsel before the first client.
**D5 (gate, cross-referenced): the first external sales of A2 and the policy component of A4** — designed in A, sold only after §5.1. They are listed here so the band map and the gate cannot drift apart again.

## 2. CROSS-CUTTING FINDINGS

1. **Liability follows custody, leverage, and profit-from-flow — not code.** Every A-band entry is non-custodial, spot-only, fee-for-service, with the client holding keys, funds, and regulated status. Every D/E entry involves holding funds or keys, leveraged products, or standing behind other people's money or prices.
