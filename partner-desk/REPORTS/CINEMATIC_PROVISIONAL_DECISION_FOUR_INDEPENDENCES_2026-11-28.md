## Binding distinction (the Guardian, adopted into the examination record)
Open-weight models eliminate per-generation provider charges — they do NOT eliminate cost. Electricity, hardware, maintenance and rendering time are costs. **The factory's unit of account is cost per ACCEPTED finished film** (all-in cost of generations ÷ films that pass acceptance), never cost per generation. This metric is now required in the multi-engine selection reconciliation (ORCH-0024 + ORCH-0027, both delivered + examined; captain's adjudication carries it).

## The four independences — permanent architectural objective
1. **Cinematic independence** — multiple hosted AND locally runnable manufacturing engines.
2. **Software independence** — exportable source code that we own, maintain and can operate independently.
3. **Intelligence independence** — multiple AI families connected through interchangeable, controlled interfaces.
4. **Infrastructure independence** — portable systems without unnecessary dependence on a single cloud provider.

An architectural objective — **not** authorization to purchase equipment or expand spending. Founder's own closing line: *"Use Google extensively where it benefits us, but never build a civilization that Google — or any other single company — can switch off."* Registered as Guardian-proposed / founder-carried; FVR candidacy noted for the next Foundational Vision Register reconciliation.
