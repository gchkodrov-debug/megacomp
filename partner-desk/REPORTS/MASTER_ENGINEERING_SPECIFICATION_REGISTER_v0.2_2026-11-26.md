This register is the engineering memory of the civilization. A mechanism not in this register does not get silently forgotten; a mechanism in it does not get silently upgraded — its state changes only on evidence, and the evidence is named in its entry. **This is an executable construction register, not an encyclopedia** (adjudication doctrine): every specification must ultimately connect to working software, a measurable examination, and a reusable engineering component. An entry whose test field names an aspiration rather than a measurement is defective and must be repaired at ingestion.

## 3. DOCTRINES AND TAGGING (adjudication corrections 1, 2, 3, 5 — binding on every entry)
- **Evidence tags.** Content is tagged where it matters: **[FACT]** documented and traceable · **[OBSERVABLE]** seen in behaviour, mechanism inferred · **[PROPOSAL]** a partner's design proposal, not house doctrine until adopted · **[ESTIMATE]** an unmeasured figure (costs, milliseconds, frame rates) — it remains an estimate until a measurement replaces it · **[SOURCE-UNVERIFIED]** an external attribution (e.g. Ubisoft's Anvil engine, GDC presentations) for which **no traceable source is attached** — such content is never presented as documented fact until a source lands.
- **Performance language.** No entry guarantees performance. Every frame-rate, byte-budget, or latency figure is a **TARGET — TO BE MEASURED ON PHYSICAL DEVICES** (FVR-31 discipline; PIS-01's decoded-memory and thermal legs included). Gemini's "guarantees 60 fps / ≤ 1.5 MB" phrasings (10 occurrences of "60 fps" in his report) were struck at ingestion, not copied.
- **Tests vs immersion.** *Automated tests prove individual mechanisms; human examination determines whether the experience genuinely feels like a living cinematic world. Neither substitutes for the other.* A VERIFIED state certifies the mechanism named in the entry — never the whole experience, and never a whole architecture.
- **Security scope.** Security entries state their evidence scope exactly. Finding no embedded secrets in a bundle certifies that bundle, not the commerce and Web3 security architecture (adjudication correction 5; see Appendix B, C-2).

## 4. THE SEVEN CLASSIFICATION STATES (the directive's, in house terms)
1. **VERIFIED** — built, and independently confirmed (blind verifier, captain's re-run of another seat's evidence, or live customer-eye proof). The confirming evidence is cited in the entry.
2. **BUILT–UNVERIFIED** — built and self-tested by its builder; the Relay has not closed.
3. **UNDER CONSTRUCTION** — a seat is actively building it now.
4. **DESIGNED** — specified on paper (architecture paper, register, directive, design file, or an ingested partner dossier); no build exists.
5. **MISSING** — known to be needed; nothing designed, nothing built.
6. **NOT APPLICABLE** — examined and ruled out for a browser civilization, with the reason recorded.
7. **RESEARCH** — requires further research before it can even be classified.

**Defect records.** Four entries carry their open defect as the state phrase — under the Zero Known-Defect Rule a known defect blocks any PASS, so the defect *is* the entry's state until it closes and is independently retested: MES-MAN-005 · MES-MOB-004 · MES-SEC-002 · MES-ECO-004. They are tallied separately in §0 and take a canonical state when their defect closes. (A fifth defect entry, MES-MAN-004, carries BUILT–UNVERIFIED with its defect noted, as in v0.1.)

**Entry template** (the directive's ten questions, as fields; compact seed lines carry state · what it is · evidence · the gap · the test): every **active** entry (VERIFIED / BUILT–UNVERIFIED / UNDER CONSTRUCTION) additionally carries, per adjudication correction 6: **Impl path** (where the working software lives or will live) · **Deps** (what it depends on) · **Evidence** (the reference that supports its state) · **Owner** (the seat/system that will verify it or keep its verification current).
