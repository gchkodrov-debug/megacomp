## §0 · THE VERDICT IN ONE PAGE

Pi Network is two different achievements wearing one name, and the founder's separation is the study's verdict:

- **As a community machine, Pi is one of the most successful builds in crypto history.** A five-second daily ritual, a social trust graph, and a single-level referral boost carried a claimed 60M+ people through six years in which the asset had no price, no transferability and no use — and still held 16.57M of them long enough to complete government-ID KYC and migrate to mainnet. No paid acquisition has ever been disclosed.
- **As an economy, on public evidence, Pi is unproven to the point of absence.** Seven years in: ~20–32K daily active wallets (a single secondary estimate plus independent on-chain censuses — direction solid, precision borrowed, see §1.1) against 60M claimed engaged; no published merchant commerce volume for any period (the one published PI-denominated activity is the .pi domains auction — escrowed bids, not commerce, see §1.4); a flagship "300+ Mainnet apps" claim that is unverifiable from Pi's own directory, whose Mainnet filter showed far fewer listed apps during summit week (directory listing and Mainnet deployment are separate gates, see §3); a token down ~98% from its February 2025 peak under continuing free-float expansion — new migrations plus Core Team/Foundation/liquidity flows, with lockup expiries of ~129M PI/month already counted inside circulating supply — into ~$5M/day of volume, with no Binance or Coinbase listing (it does trade on Kraken, OKX, Gate, Bitget and MEXC). The programmable economy — DEX, AMM, Launchpad, third-party tokens, subscriptions, x402 — exists today **only on Testnet**, with Mainnet targeted for Q1 2027.

**For PROVADIA the lesson is exact:** Pi built the crowd first and is now, in year seven, trying to retrofit an economy underneath it — while its crowd's tokens bleed value monthly. Our build order is the reverse and must stay the reverse: genuine utility and first receipts while the community is small enough that every citizen can be a customer. What we adopt from Pi is its *retention engineering* (the daily meaningful act, the trust graph) — never its *monetary engineering* (issuance-funded boosts, lockup multipliers, referral rate), which our fixed supply forbids anyway and its own evidence condemns.

## §2 · THE RETURN MECHANICS, DISSECTED

*(Seat B's memo, adjudicated; screen evidence from the founder's own app added — Appendix A. Pi has never published audited DAU/MAU, churn, or session data; retention evidence is therefore the KYC/migration funnel, Sensor Tower regional panels, and on-chain activity.)*

### 2.1 The 24-hour mining session — the load-bearing mechanic

**Mechanics.** Tap the lightning button; a 24-hour session runs; on expiry mining stops until the next tap. The phone performs no proof-of-work — the tap is an attendance/liveness attestation ("confirms daily that they are not a robot"). The base rate B began at **3.1415926 π/h** (14 Mar 2019) and halved at each 10× growth milestone (1K/10K/100K/1M/10M engaged) to 0.09817 π/h. On **1 Mar 2022** the milestone model was replaced by a declining-exponential issuance formula: B is recalculated monthly as a fixed pool divided by the sum of all active miners' coefficients — *more concurrent miners, lower B for everyone*. Tracked B: ~0.0237 (Mar 2022) → 0.0140 (Jan 2023) → ~0.0032 (Jan 2025) → **0.0027405 π/h (Sep 2025)** — at base rate, ~24 π/year, roughly fifteen days to mine a single Pi. The full rate formula is `M = B • (1 + S + L) • (1 + N + E + A + X)` — Security Circle and Lockup multiply everything else, including Referral, Node and App rewards. The founder's own home screen (App. A) displays his total rate as **0.003 π/h** — the collapsed base. (Frame-order note: the four screenshots span 12:02–12:03; the explainer overlay shows the bolt at 0 π/h with the session expired, and the home frame shows 0.003 π/h — the session appears to have been restarted between frames; "still ticking" is an inference from that sequence, not a single-frame observation.)

**Psychology.** The session *stops* — the counter visibly halts — so not returning is framed as losing income already being earned, not failing to gain something new (loss aversion). Seven years of declining B added manufactured scarcity ("mine now, tomorrow pays less" was literally true). The streak is denominated in a number that only goes up (endowed progress). Compliance costs ~5 seconds — the lowest-friction daily ritual ever attached to a financial asset.

**Retention evidence.** The funnel (§1.1) is the hard evidence: at most ~27% of the claimed base ever completed the return-heavy KYC/migration path. Sensor Tower regional panels show event-driven spikes then 20–35% quarterly decay (Europe actives ~5.8M at the Feb 2025 launch peak → ~4.5M a month later; Nigeria Android actives 2.15M → 1.62M across Q4 2025). No earlier-year DAU baseline exists — Pi never disclosed one in 2019–2024 either.

**Honest cost.** Sessions pay from the pre-allocated 65B mining pool — capped, but a continuous circulating-supply inflation (~1.21B PI scheduled into circulation across 2026). The ritual also monetised attention for years before the token had any value (in-app ads against unwithdrawable balances — a persistent, documented user grievance).

### 2.2 Security circles — a trust graph wearing a security uniform

**Mechanics (verified against the whitepaper).** Unlocks after 3 mining sessions. Add 3–5 people you personally know; `S(B) = 0.2 • min(Sc, 5) • B` — **+20% of B per member, capped at +100%** — and because S sits inside the multiplier, a full circle doubles Referral, Node and App rewards too. The founder's modal (App. A) shows the mechanic from inside: contribution graded **WEAK** (red bar) at **+0.001 π/h** with 2 of 5 members (40%) — even a "weak" circle is a third of his displayed 0.003 π/h total.

**The technical truth (the 2019 whitepaper's own words, in substance).** Pi adapts the Stellar Consensus Protocol, where *validator Nodes* choose quorum slices and safety requires quorum intersection. Pi's stated contribution is that the contributor trust graph is "information that can be used by the Pi nodes when they are setting up their configurations." Therefore: **(1)** phones and circles validate nothing — consensus runs on Nodes; **(2)** a circle does not secure your account — the wallet passphrase does, and it is irrecoverable; **(3)** a circle does not by itself secure the chain — and secondary reporting through 2026 states Mainnet validators are reportedly all Core-Team-operated, making the trust graph's decentralising function largely theoretical today; **(4)** the Sybil resistance that actually shipped was **centralised KYC**, not circles. The app's own copy — "you help secure the Pi transaction history by setting up your security circle" (App. A) — overstates a configuration hint for node operators into a consensus role users do not have.

**Psychology & real work.** Circles convert a solitary tap into a social obligation to named friends, make users perform Pi's trust-labelling labour for free in exchange for a doubling, and create commitment (abandoning the app means abandoning a structure you built). Socially they did genuine work: they bootstrapped a dense real-world trust graph and forced recruitment through pre-existing relationships. No published data isolates circle completion vs. retention. (Arithmetic note: the modal's +0.001 π/h at 2 of 5 members implies B ≈ 0.0025 under the 0.2 × 2 × B formula — slightly below the Sep 2025 base of 0.0027405 quoted in §2.1; the difference is the app's display rounding.)

### 2.3 Lockup incentives — the mechanic that worked, and the bill

**Mechanics (whitepaper).** `L(B) = Lt • Lp • log(N) • B`, chosen voluntarily pre-migration, binding on-chain after. Duration Lt: 2 weeks 0.1 · 6 months 0.5 · 1 year 1 · **3 years 2**. Share Lp: up to **200% of the migrated balance = 2** (you may lock Pi acquired elsewhere). log(N) is the log of your total past sessions — the lockup boost *grows with your tap streak*, welding §2.1 to §2.3. The whitepaper's own worked example yields +2B for a 100%/3-year lock; headline boosts of several hundred percent are formula-possible.

**Uptake — massive.** ExplorePi data reported Mar 2025: of 11.5M migrated accounts, **7.2M (62.6%) chose the full 3-year lockup** (1.6M one-year, 1.1M six-month; the three tiers sum to 9.9M — the ~1.6M remainder held 2-week locks or none) — the majority picked the maximum, not tradable until late 2027/early 2028. Explorer dashboards (Nov 2026) put ~75% of migrated Pi **by value** locked — a different metric from the by-account figure above, consistent with ~6.0B PI locked in total. On the founder's home screen the lockup upsell banner — "Increase Lockup Boost Rate!" — is the first element under the balance (App. A): the ask is still being made, at the top of the daily surface, in Nov 2026.

**Psychology.** The choice was presented at the moment of maximum excitement (migration, after years of waiting) as a slider whose biggest number sat at the far end, denominated in more of a token that — when most users set it in 2022–2024 — **had no market price at all**. The boost was framed as a mining-rate multiplier, never as an annualised yield, keeping it outside the mental category "investment return" while functioning exactly like one.

**Honest cost.** The reward is newly issued PI from the same pool — no fee income, no external cash flow behind it; the cost is dilution transferred to all holders, including the locker. The regret is documented: the 3-year lockers watched the full round trip ($2.99 → $0.05) contractually immobile, and by Mar 2025 an **account-selling black market** existed specifically to escape lockups (whole accounts sold with wallet passphrases — a ToS violation and a theft risk). On the legal framing, precision matters: **no SEC/CFTC action exists, and no regulator or court has formally characterised the lockup boost as an unregistered-securities yield.** What exists: critic arguments (Justin Bons, Mar 2025: "Ponzi-like"), a **US federal lawsuit filed 24 Oct 2025 (N.D. Cal. 5:25-cv-09145-NC)** — a single named Arizona plaintiff seeking $10M (no class certification appears in the record reached) — alleging a fraudulent scheme and secret sales of ~2B PI. Pi stated on the record that it "did not conduct a secret sale of 2 billion Pi tokens" and "vehemently denies" the allegations. **The court GRANTED the defendants' motion to dismiss, without leave to amend, on 13 May 2026 (Document 52)** — the case is dismissed, not pending. Pi's MiCA whitepaper sits in ESMA's interim register, which is a filing, not an approval.

### 2.4 Referral / team mechanics — single-level pay, multi-level shape

**Mechanics (verified).** One inviter per person, set once by code (= the inviter's username). `E(I) = Ec • 0.25 • I` — inviter and invitee each get an equal **25% boost** per team member *mining concurrently*; **no cap**. The app lets you "ping" inactive members — Pi outsources its push notifications to users' friendships. The concurrency term is the genius detail: your income depends on your team tapping *today, at the same time as you*, converting every user into an unpaid retention manager. (The founder's rail shows his earning team at 0/4 — App. A.)

**A correction the record requires:** the rewards are **explicitly single-level** — "your invitee's invitees don't affect you at all," nobody pays anything to join, and the split is equal. What is multi-level is the *growth graph* (invitation chains propagate virally across generations), not the payment cascade. That distinction is Pi's strongest legal defence, and any honest study must state it before criticising elsewhere.

**The catch that compounded.** Referral-bonus Pi was **excluded from first migrations**; it migrates only in second migrations, and only for team members who themselves fully passed KYC — bonuses from accounts judged fake are deleted. Years of displayed balances were therefore contingent in a way the headline number never showed (a documented driver of the Mar 2025 KYC-deadline panic), and by late Mar 2026 only ~119K Pioneers had completed a second migration.

**Growth evidence.** Referral-only distribution reached 10M engaged (Dec 2020) → 30M+ (Dec 2021) → 55M (Jun 2023) → **100M app downloads (Nov 2025)** with no paid acquisition ever disclosed. No credible source publishes a measured viral coefficient; the honest statement is that referral-only growth to 100M installs implies sustained k near or above replacement for long periods, concentrated in Nigeria, India, Vietnam, Indonesia, Pakistan.

**Honest cost — the mercenary problem.** Zero cost basis = zero sell resistance: referred users paid nothing, risked nothing, and for years could not sell; when selling opened (20 Feb 2025) the rational mercenary move was immediate exit, and the price path (§1.2) fits that model. "Tap-to-earn fatigue" shows in behaviour, not just commentary: the account-selling market, the KYC panic, Sensor Tower's post-spike decay, and 0.03–0.05% daily on-chain activity describe a base *recruited as miners of a free asset, not onboarded as users of a payments network*. Jurisdictional exposure followed the shape, not the formula: Bybit's CEO ("still think you are a scam," Feb–Mar 2025), Vietnamese police warnings (Mar 2025) that Pi is not legal payment, and a Dec 2024 joint warning by seven Chinese financial associations naming Pi as a prop criminals use for illegal fundraising/pyramid pretexts (a warning about criminals' use of Pi in a jurisdiction that bans crypto generally — not a court finding that Pi is a pyramid scheme; Pi publicly rejects the MLM label, and no US regulator has acted).

### 2.5 Seat B's verdict, adopted

**The 24-hour session did the retention work, and it is not close.** Every other mechanic is a multiplier on the session and dies without it — circles pay only miners, referrals pay only on concurrent sessions, and the lockup formula literally contains log(N), the count of past sessions. A five-second ritual carried a claimed 60M people across six years with no price, no transferability and no use, and still held 16M+ through KYC and migration after the token fell 98% and the base yield collapsed to a fraction of a cent a day. Referrals were the greatest *acquisition* engine; lockups the greatest *supply-suppression* engine; neither is retention. **Pi's real invention was making the return visit itself the asset-generating act, cheap enough to survive the total collapse of every other reason to return.** PROVADIA's question is what the return visit generates when it cannot generate issuance — §4 answers: it must generate *accomplishment the citizen can point to*.

## §5 · THE COMMUNITY ACQUISITION ENGINE — HOW PI GOT MILLIONS WITHOUT A PURCHASE

*(Extension dimension 1. Funnel honesty is mandatory in this section: the four populations are kept separate throughout.)*

### 5.1 The engine, part by part

**Zero price of entry, zero skill floor.** Pi never asked for money, hardware, or knowledge. Install the app, create an account, tap once — the first "reward" (a growing balance number) arrives within the first session. Compare the crypto norm of the era: buy on an exchange, custody a seed phrase, pay gas. Pi removed every step between curiosity and ownership-feeling. The onboarding cost was an app install and a phone number/Facebook login; the first reward cost one tap.

**The referral graph as the only marketing department.** One inviter code per person (= username), an equal 25% boost to both sides per concurrently-mining member, no cap (§2.4). Growth ran through pre-existing trust — family, coworkers, village networks — which is why the map of Pi's strength (Nigeria, India, Vietnam, Indonesia, Pakistan per Sensor Tower rankings) follows diaspora and word-of-mouth corridors, not advertising markets. No paid acquisition has ever been disclosed; referral-only distribution reached 100M Google Play installs (Nov 2025).

**Daily participation design.** The 24h session (§2.1) converted acquisition into habit at near-zero friction, and the boosts made the habit *social*: your rate visibly depends on your circle (§2.2) and your team's concurrency (§2.4), so the app recruits its own reminder system — users ping each other. The home screen (App. A) is the whole engine on one surface: balance on top, lockup upsell, four rail readouts that are each either a reward or an incomplete task, and a feed that carries the next announcement.

**International expansion pattern.** Mobile-first, low-bandwidth-tolerant, free, and denominated in hope rather than dollars: Pi spread where a phone is the only computer and a dollar-denominated entry would have excluded everyone. The mining framing travelled across languages without localisation of the core loop — tap, invite, wait. The same pattern is also the mercenary trap's geography: the user bases Pi won are precisely those for whom a free asset that might become money is the strongest possible pitch, and for whom the eventual lifetime base-rate mining total — on the order of tens of dollars at Nov 2026 prices (an order-of-magnitude illustration from the §2.1 rate path, not a measured per-user figure) — is the cruelest version of the disappointment.

## §6 · THE MOBILE APPLICATION STRATEGY — SHOULD PROVADIA HAVE AN APP?

*(Extension dimension 2. Strategy assessment only; no development is authorized or proposed for build.)*

### 6.1 What app-first actually bought Pi

Everything Pi is, runs through the app: the mining tap exists only there; the feed (its announcement channel — App. A shows the summit post inside the app) exists only there; the wallet lives in the companion Pi Browser; push re-engagement, identity, and the directory are app surfaces. The app bought Pi: **(1)** the daily habit's home — an icon on the phone is a permanent billboard no website gets; **(2)** distribution — 100M+ Google Play installs, store search as a discovery channel; **(3)** a captive channel — every announcement reaches the base without media, email, or an algorithm in between; **(4)** the KYC funnel's container. What it did *not* buy: an economy (§1.4) — the app is a superb container for a ritual and a feed, and the ritual and the feed are what filled it.

The costs Pi pays for app-first are equally concrete: two app-store gatekeepers between Pi and its users; update friction (features wait on store review and user updates — Pi's own Protocol deadlines are enforced through app/node updates); a permanent surface for the scam-prop impersonation problem (fake Pi apps are a documented genre); and the strategic trap visible in App. A — **when the app is the product, the home screen optimises for return visits, not for transactions.** There is no commerce ask anywhere on Pi's primary surface.

### 6.2 The three roads for PROVADIA, compared

| | Native app | PWA (installable web app) | Our existing web infrastructure |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cost** | Highest — two platforms or a cross-platform build, store compliance, ongoing store-policy maintenance; a second codebase forever | Low — one web codebase, install prompt, offline shell, push (with platform limits, esp. iOS) | Already built and shipping — the living website organism (15 pages, doors, films) exists tonight |
| **Distribution** | Store search + install icon; but store review gates every release, crypto apps face heightened scrutiny, and a token-linked app invites policy risk we cannot control | No store gate; installs from our own pages; no store search presence | Link-native: every door is a URL, shareable through the Nexus today; no install step at all |
| **Update friction** | Worst — review queues + user update lag; a fix is a negotiation | None — deploy is live (our deploy law already works this way) | None — the house's whole build/deploy discipline is web-native |
| **The daily-habit surface** | Strongest — home-screen icon + native push | Good — icon + push where supported | Weakest — return depends on bookmarks, Discord, the Day-Book's rhythm |
| **Fit with our machinery** | Poor today — our Surfer films, Glass Machinery, Day-Book recompute and Nexus are web builds; an app would fork them | Strong — wraps the same organism | Native — it *is* the organism |

### 6.3 Assessment

**Not now, and not native first.** The honest sequence: (1) the web organism is the civilization — finish it (it is mid-build tonight, ready count ZERO; an app fork now would split the crew across two surfaces before one is finished); (2) when citizens exist and a daily citizen act exists (§4.2), wrap the organism as a **PWA** — the citizen's phone gets the icon and the daily beat without a store gate between the town and its people; (3) a native app earns consideration only when a capability the web genuinely cannot deliver becomes load-bearing (secure enclave key custody for a house wallet product is the only candidate on the horizon, and no such product is founded). Pi's evidence cuts both ways and both cuts land here: the app made the *habit* — and the habit is the one Pi organ we are adopting (§4.2) — but Pi also proves an app cannot manufacture the economy the habit was supposed to serve. Build the economy on the web we control; borrow the app's habit surface via PWA when there are citizens to habituate.

→ **Our fence:** the Charter + Statutes pattern (FVR-23) and the Day-Book itself — rules are published objects, changes are recorded entries, and the Umbrella's terms (V2, NO TITHE) were *settled in the open* after the tithe version was replaced. **Rule for the developer regime (§7.2): terms an approved house joins under cannot be changed retroactively against it** — the Graduated Door punishes fraud, not reliance.

**F-10 · The transparency tool went dark.** PiScan, the main independent explorer, has reportedly been offline since 7 Sep 2026 — through the entire summit season — with no explanation found (Seat A). When the ecosystem's watcher is a volunteer, scrutiny is optional.
→ **Our fence:** the Verifier is founded as *ours to maintain and everyone's to run* (open script, off-server proof packs) — scrutiny of PROVADIA must not depend on PROVADIA's uptime or goodwill, including ours.

**What is NOT on this list, deliberately:** the price itself. A −98% token with 16.57M humans who completed KYC and migration is not a dead project — it is a live community carrying a broken monetary design. (That they completed KYC and migration is the record; how many still open the app daily is the ~20–32K question of §1.1, and the two must never be fused.) Pi's failure set is specific, and every item on it is a *design or communication choice*, which is exactly why it is preventable.

### ADOPT (with the adaptation)

| Announcing the economy layer years before it ships | §3 — Launchpad/DEX/tokens are Q1 2027 *targets*; the announcement is not the asset |

### The founder's separation, as the study's verdict

**Pi succeeded at attracting a community and is struggling to create a sustainable economy; those are two different achievements, and Pi has exactly one of them.** PROVADIA's founding bet is the reverse order — utility while the community grows, so that people have real reasons to become customers as well as citizens. This study's evidence says the bet is correctly placed and gives it a clock: Pi's crowd took ~2 years to reach 10M on referral mechanics alone, but its economy is *still* unbuilt in year seven. A small civilization that sells real services in its first year is ahead of Pi on the only axis that compounds.
