    4. Full Product Description (The Whole Nine Yards)
    What It Is
    
    AI Agent Fleet — Done-For-You Install is a one-time installation service performed by OneJobKits for small business owners (2 to 50 staff) in e-commerce, local services, clinics, and agencies who field repetitive customer messages, follow-ups, or bookings by hand—or who want AI automation with strict visibility, cost caps, and an off-switch they actually trust.
    
    This service is not software, not a subscription, not a monthly retainer, and not the boxed DIY #511 kit. We build the working fleet inside your own tools on your own accounts and API keys, then hand you the keys and command records so you run it unaided.
    
    What You Receive — The 7-Part Installation Process
    
    Every engagement follows seven documented steps in exact order:
    
    The Intake: A 20-question written intake questionnaire that fixes your exact scope, chosen agents, and tool boundaries before any building begins.
    
    The Map: A channel-by-channel chart of your repetitive customer questions, follow-ups, and bookings translated into a named list of jobs—separating what an agent handles from what stays human.
    
    The Training Material: Your own policies, price lists, FAQs, and past answers structured into clean reference files, with every missing policy or pricing gap flagged by name rather than filled by guesswork.
    
    The Fleet: One to three working AI agents (Support, Follow-Up, and Scheduling/Admin—1 in Starter, 2 to 3 in Fleet and Command, fixed at intake) built inside your own business tools and accounts.
    
    The Guardrails: Owner-defined rules enforcing approved actions, money limits (such as a refund ceiling), automatic inbox escalation when a question falls outside scope, and an individual kill switch for every agent.
    
    The Command Records (#511 Control Layer): Full setup of Fleet_Command_Center.xlsx for your business—containing your Agent Roster & Roles Register, Per-Agent Cost Caps & Spend Tracker, QA Scorecard for sampling real customer answers, and Weekly Review Kill/Keep Board.
    
    The Handover: A live closing session ending in a practical test where you operate your fleet and trigger a kill switch unaided, sign the written delivery checklist, and activate your 30-day support window.
    
    Tier Comparison
    
    Starter ($495 USD / $396 in PROVADIA): Complete 7-part install for 1 AI Agent (Support, Follow-Up, or Scheduling/Admin) + #511 Command Records + live handover + 30-day support window.
    
    Fleet ($795 USD / $636 in PROVADIA): Complete 7-part install for 2 to 3 AI Agents + #511 Command Records + live handover + 30-day support window.
    
    Command ($995 USD / $796 in PROVADIA): Everything in Fleet, plus 1x Weekly Kill/Keep Review set up and run with you, a Live Team Training Session, and a 30-Day Tuning Pass.
    
    Before Install vs. After Handover
    
    Before Install: Customer questions are answered by hand or missed; follow-ups live in the owner's head; nothing is logged when an automated tool speaks to a customer; AI usage spend stays invisible until a bill arrives.
    
    After Handover: Your fleet answers strictly from your own documents inside your own tools; anything outside your guardrails lands in your inbox with the full story; one roster shows every agent, its permissions, cost cap, and spend; the QA scorecard records sampled real answers with scores; you operate every kill switch unaided.
    
    What Happens After Purchase
    
    You receive the Start Here PDF and the 20-Question Intake Questionnaire immediately upon order confirmation.
    
    Once you return the completed intake and source documents, we map your channels, build your agents inside your accounts (we never hold or store your passwords), configure your guardrails and #511 Command Records, and schedule your live handover test.
    
    At the handover test, you operate the system unaided, sign the delivery checklist, and begin your 30-day post-handover support window.
    
    Terms, Boundaries & What We Never Promise
    
    Usage Costs: Your agents run on your own accounts and API keys; ongoing provider usage fees are paid directly by you and fenced by your per-agent cost caps.
    
    No Accuracy, Revenue, or Time-Savings Guarantees: AI agents can make mistakes; our guardrails, QA scorecard, and kill switches exist specifically to catch and bound errors. We make zero promises or claims regarding hours saved, money saved, or revenue gained.
    
    Support & Refund Terms: Post-handover support runs for 30 calendar days (the window is the promise). Refunds apply specifically to any named deliverable in your scope that is not delivered—there is no blanket money-back guarantee.
    
    Capacity & Live Status: Limited to 2 to 3 installs per month. Currently not live (checkout is not connected).
