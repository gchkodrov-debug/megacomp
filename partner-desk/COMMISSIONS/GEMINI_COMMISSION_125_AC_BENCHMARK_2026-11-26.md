## For each mechanism, the directive's ten questions
What does it do · how does it work · why does it matter · can it serve PROVADIA · can it operate inside a browser · reusable across every universe · dependencies/costs/performance · have we built it (your best knowledge — the Captain's audit settles our side) · what is missing · how can it be objectively tested.

## Deliverable
A research report structured for direct ingestion into the new **Master Engineering Specification Register**: one entry per mechanism with a proposed ID block (the Captain assigns final IDs at ingestion), the ten-question answers, and your classification recommendation in the directive's seven states. Flag the mechanisms you judge HIGHEST-LEVERAGE for a browser civilization first — the ones whose absence would hurt most.
