## THE RESEARCH AS CARRIED BY THE FOUNDER (ChatGPT's preliminary findings)

Evidence that gaming and commerce can coexist:
- Roblox + Shopify: customers can discover and purchase real merchandise inside playable worlds.
- Roblox digital rewards: real purchases can include virtual items and benefits.
- Axie Infinity: financial incentives can attract players, but dependence on token appreciation can undermine sustainability.
- Web3 gaming research: bots and reward farming are genuine risks.
- Roblox reported 97.8 million average daily users when announcing its expanded commerce integration in May 2025 — scale of the opportunity; its Shopify channel currently excludes digital products.

Four models proposed for investigation:
1. Explore-to-discover: find real products through adventures.
2. Learn-to-unlock: complete challenges and gain useful knowledge.
3. Earn-to-save: unlock carefully controlled discounts.
4. Proof of achievement: optional Web3 digital collectibles representing genuine accomplishments.
These could coexist without turning PROVADIA into a speculative play-to-earn game.

The biggest unanswered question (the research's own admission): whether gaming audiences would become genuine customers of our software. The research establishes precedent, not guaranteed demand or profitability.
