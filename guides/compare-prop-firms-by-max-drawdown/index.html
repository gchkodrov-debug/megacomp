How do I compare prop firms by max drawdown before buying a challenge?

Traders shop for challenges on the drawdown number — but the headline percentage is the weakest way to compare. Two firms can both advertise “10% max drawdown” while one gives you a fixed floor and the other a floor that trails your best day, which are entirely different challenges. Compare the mechanics underneath the percentage, firm by firm, on one sheet — before any money moves.

What do I write down for each firm?

  • Challenge price and account size.
  • Max drawdown percentage — and the actual dollar floor it creates on day one.
  • Daily loss limit percentage — and its dollar floor.
  • Floor type: static, intraday trailing, or EOD trailing.
  • Measured on balance or equity — and whether open P&L counts.
  • Profit target percentage.
  • Any time limit on the evaluation.
  • Payout split, and the conditions for the first payout.
  • Where the full written rules live — link, plus the date you saved them.

Our guide on floor types and open P&L explains each mechanic in plain words.

What should I check beyond the numbers?

Evidence. Traders regularly ask of a firm's claims: “are you sure — is there evidence?” Apply that before paying: if a benefit appears in marketing but not in the written rules, treat the written rules as the deal. Save the rule page on the day you compare, so the comparison you made is the one on record if the page changes later — that habit is the start of your evidence folder.
