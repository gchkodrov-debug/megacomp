Workshop-in-a-Box for Coaches & Consultants — workshop planning kit

Your expertise is the course. This is everything around it. Anyone who has taught a live session knows the teaching is the smallest part of the work: there is a deck to design, a workbook for attendees to write in, a session to time minute by minute, a launch to run across four weeks, and a trail of follow-up emails afterwards. A complete workshop planning kit puts all of that stationery in one designed set — so the next time you teach, you start from a finished system instead of a blank slide and a fortnight of evenings.

The pain it solves is rebuild fatigue. Coaches and consultants re-teach their material for years, yet the deck gets remade for every audience, the run-of-show lives in a notebook, the launch checklist is reinvented per event, and the follow-up emails get written at midnight after the session, or not at all. This kit is designed once and used every time: twenty named slides in a fixed teaching order, a workbook that mirrors the session, a planner that times it, a checklist that runs the four weeks before it, and scripts that handle the week after it.

What this kit is: templates and stationery — designed, structured, ready for your words. What it is not: it is not a course, and it contains no teaching content of any kind. Every slide and workbook page carries replace-me placeholder text for your material; the kit makes no income, attendance or outcome promises, and its email scripts ship with honesty rules — real numbers, real testimonials with permission — as a feature, not fine print.
