Questions buyers ask

Are these really editable in Canva?
Yes. Every design is supplied as a PNG and as an editable SVG. Open the SVG in Canva or in any vector tool, replace the photo placeholder with your listing photo, change the words and numbers to yours, and export. The Canva Usage Guide walks through that short workflow step by step.
Do the designs include photos or market statistics?
No. Photo areas are placeholders for your own listing photos, and the market stat boxes ship empty on purpose. You fill them with your own MLS or board numbers each month. A template that arrived with somebody else's statistics would be a liability, not a shortcut, so this kit never includes market data.
How many designs do I get, and what does Pro add?
Base has 25 designs in Classic Navy and Gold: 12 feed posts, 4 stories, a 5-slide market update carousel, 2 flyers and 2 postcards, plus the print pack and the usage guide. Pro adds 15 more designs — 40 in total — including 8 core designs re-drawn in Charcoal and Champagne, Closing Day, Referral Ask, Home Anniversary and Seasonal Greeting posts, a door hanger, and a business card front and back.
Can I use the Testimonial design with any words I like?
Only with real words and permission. The testimonial template is for genuine client testimonials that the client has agreed to let you use. Inventing a testimonial, or using one without permission, is exactly what the usage guide tells you not to do — the design is a frame for a real quote, never a substitute for one.
What are the fair housing rules for these templates?
Describe homes, never people. Your copy should talk about the property, the rooms, the location and the facts of the listing — not about who should live there or what kind of buyer or neighbour to expect. The usage guide carries this rule into the kit itself, and it applies to every post, flyer and postcard you make from these files.
What does the licence allow?
You may use the templates for your own marketing for as long as you market — your listings, your open houses, your market updates, in print and online. You may not resell the template files, share them with other agents as files, or pass them off as a product of your own. Your finished posts and printed pieces are yours; the underlying template files stay licensed, not sold.
