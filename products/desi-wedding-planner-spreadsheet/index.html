Couples — and the parents helping them — organising a multi-day South Asian wedding in the US, UK, Canada or Australia, who want the budgets, guests, vendors, outfits, travel and family split in one planner instead of five notebooks and a group chat.

Questions buyers ask

How is this different from a generic wedding planner spreadsheet?
A generic planner assumes one ceremony, one reception and one guest list. A South Asian wedding is three, four, five events. This planner is built by event: Mehndi, Haldi, Sangeet, Ceremony and Reception each get their own budget, guest list, vendor list and run sheet — and everything rolls up into one master budget, so you plan the way the wedding actually happens.
Does it cover Hindu, Sikh and Muslim ceremonies?
Yes — there are three dedicated ceremony organiser tabs (Hindu Ceremony, Sikh Ceremony, Muslim Ceremony) for structuring the ceremony itself, covering weddings that include a Nikkah or an Anand Karaj as well as the shared events around them. They are planning organisers only: your family and your officiant are the authority on your traditions; the tabs just give every detail a home.
Two families are splitting the costs — how does that work?
The Cash Flow & Split tab exists for exactly this: it takes each event's actual spending, applies each family's percentage share, and works out the settlement — who has paid what so far and who owes whom to square it. The conversation still has to happen; the arithmetic no longer has to be argued.
Excel or Google Sheets?
Both formats are included — the workbook runs in Microsoft Excel and in Google Sheets with the same 34 tabs and formulas, so both families can work from the same numbers wherever they are.
How do vendor payments stay under control across five events?
One Vendor Payments tab holds every vendor across all events — deposits paid, balances, and due dates — and the Phone View pulls the money onto one screen: what is due in the next 30 days and what is overdue right now, so a payment deadline is never discovered the week of the wedding.
What about guests flying in — travel and hotels?
The Travel & Hotels tab tracks arrivals, rooms and who needs picking up — the diaspora logistics generic planners never model — and the Master Guest List keeps one list for the whole wedding with per-event RSVPs, so the Sangeet headcount and the Reception headcount stop being the same guess.
What does the free starter cover, and what does the full planner add?
The free starter is one tab: the master budget layout set up for a single event (the Sangeet), so you can see exactly how the per-event system works. The full planner runs all five events — budgets, guests, vendors, run sheets — plus outfits & jewellery, vendor payments, the family split, travel & hotels, the gift & shagun log and the ceremony tabs. The Bundle adds the printable run-sheet pack and the vendor email templates.
